About

From the precision of CNC machining to software engineering — a career built on logic, rigor and attention to detail.

I hold a degree in Full-Stack Development and a postgraduate degree in Software Engineering. I work end to end, from front-end to back-end, and my focus today is software architecture: designing systems that are scalable, well structured and built to evolve.

I currently work on real-world projects involving SaaS platforms, ERP systems and applied Artificial Intelligence — LLMs, agents, RAG with vector databases and integrations through the MCP (Model Context Protocol) that connect AI to business data and processes.

Before software, I worked in industrial automation and CNC programming. That background gave me process discipline and a careful eye for quality, which I bring to every line of code.
